## Game Rules

- All wins shown in credits, except Jackpots. Touch CREDIT, BET or WIN meter to show in currency or credits.
- 50 lines are played. Choose your bet multiplier.
- The Bet buttons and the BIG button will immediately commence play.
- All wins on played lines only, except Jackpot feature prizes, Free Games prizes and Ghost feature prizes.
- Wins on different lines are added. Highest win only on each line.
- Line wins are multiplied by the bet multiplier.
- Free Games prizes, Ghost feature prizes and Jackpots are added to line wins.
- The green, red or blue diamond level does not influence chance of triggering any feature.
- The gold coin level in Free Games and Jackpot chests does not influence the chance of triggering any feature.
- Malfunction voids all pays and plays. Player is responsible to confirm credits registered before game start.

## Ghost Features

- Ghost with one or more green diamond awards Mo' Spins feature.
- Ghost with one or more red diamond awards Go Ghost feature.
- Ghost with one or more blue diamond awards Mo' Symbols feature.
- Ghost with one or more green and red diamond awards Mo' Spins Go Ghost feature.
- Ghost with one or more red and blue diamond awards Go Ghost Mo' Symbols feature.
- Ghost with one or more green and blue diamond awards Mo' Spins Mo' Symbols feature.
- Ghost with one or more green, red and blue diamond awards Mo' Spins Go Ghost Mo' Symbols feature.

- One or more green diamond in a spin without Ghost may randomly trigger Mo' Spins feature.
- One or more red diamond in a spin without Ghost may randomly trigger Go Ghost feature.
- One or more blue diamond in a spin without Ghost may randomly trigger Mo' Symbols feature.
- If only Mo' Spins feature is triggered randomly in the spin, Mo' Spins feature is awarded.
- If only Go Ghost feature is triggered randomly in the spin, Go Ghost feature is awarded.
- If only Mo' Symbols feature is triggered randomly in the spin, Mo' Symbols feature is awarded.
- If only Mo' Spins and Go Ghost features are triggered randomly in the spin, Mo' Spins Go Ghost feature is awarded.
- If only Go Ghost and Mo' Symbols features are triggered randomly in the spin, Go Ghost Mo' Symbols feature is awarded.
- If only Mo' Spins and Mo' Symbols features are triggered randomly in the spin, Mo' Spins Mo' Symbols feature is awarded.
- If Mo' Spins, Go Ghost and Mo' Symbols features are triggered randomly in the spin, Mo' Spins Go Ghost Mo' Symbols feature is awarded.

- Every green, red and blue diamond has an associated prize displayed on it.
- Possible prizes: (0.5-100) x Total Bet, Grand, Major, Maxi, Minor or Mini.
- The odds of credit prizes change with the bet multiplier and denomination.
- The odds of credit prizes change from bought games to Ghost features.
- At most one Grand may appear on green, red or blue diamond in a spin.
- At the start of any Ghost feature, all green, red and blue diamond in the triggering spin convert into light-blue diamond with same prize displayed on them and copied to the corresponding positions.
- During any Ghost feature, all positions spin individually.
- 5 spins are awarded initially. Bonus reels are in play.
- At the start of any Ghost feature, 2x2 Ghost appears and moves to all positions containing light-blue diamond.
- After each feature spin, prizes displayed on the coin which land behind the Ghost are collected before the Ghost moves to positions containing the light-blue diamond.
- Prizes displayed on each light-blue diamond are also collected when the Ghost moves to their positions.
- Feature ends when all spins are completed. All collected coin and light-blue diamond prizes are awarded.
- Light-blue diamond will never land behind the Ghost of any size during the feature spins.
- The bet multiplier is the same as the game that triggered any of the Ghost features.

## Mo' Spins Feature

Feature is played on 5x5 reel matrix.

| Diamonds collected     | Ghost size | Additional spins |
| ---------------------- | ---------- | ---------------- |
| Once 5                 | 2x2 → 3x3  | 3                |
| Once 4 additional      | 3x3 → 4x4  | 2                |
| Once 3 more additional | 4x4 → 5x5  | 1                |

Once the Ghost size increases to 5x5, no more diamonds can appear.

- Additional 1 spin is awarded if coin or light-blue diamond with +1 Spin displayed on it is collected.
- Possible coin and light-blue diamond prizes: +1 Spin, (0.5-100) x Total Bet, Minor or Mini.
- The odds of credit prizes change with the bet multiplier and denomination.

## Free Games Feature

- Any 3, 4 or 5 Free Games scatter symbols trigger Free Games feature and award 6 free games along with 2x, 10x or 50x Total Bet credit prize respectively.
- Any Free Games symbol may also trigger Free Games feature and award 6 free games along with 2x Total Bet credit prize.
- Free Games symbol is scatter. Free Games feature can be triggered again during the Free Games feature.
- Bonus reels are in play during the feature.
- The bet multiplier is the same as the game that triggered the feature.

---

## Jackpot Feature

- Any WILD may randomly trigger the Jackpot feature.
- Touch any gold chest (a gold icon on a chain) to reveal GRAND, MAJOR, MAXI, MINOR or MINI.
- Match 3 to win corresponding prize.
- Outcomes of Jackpot feature are not influenced by player input.

---

## Jackpots

- Grand, Major and Maxi are progressives. Minor and Mini are fixed prizes.
- Jackpot meter amount is reset each time it has been paid, before any other Jackpots are paid.
- Once the Jackpot ceiling amount has been reached, further contributions are added to subsequent Jackpot progressives.
- Jackpots are not multiplied by the bet multiplier.
- An increase in wager increases the odds of winning progressives proportionally.
- The odds of Minor and Mini change with the bet multiplier.
- The odds of Minor and Mini change with payline options.
- The odds of jackpots change from bought games to Ghost features.
- The odds of jackpots change from bought games to free games.
